# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind, assume good faith, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing other
people's private information are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by messaging a member of the [Coding Folks](https://github.com/Coding-Folks) organisation. Reports
are handled confidentially, and maintainers who do not act in line with this code may be removed.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
